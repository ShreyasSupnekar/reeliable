PUT YOUR 5 ADS IN THIS FOLDER
=============================

Name them like this:

  work1.mp4   <- your BEST ad (shown big)
  work2.mp4
  work3.mp4
  work4.mp4
  work5.mp4

Images also work (work1.jpg, work2.png ...). If you use a different
name or file type, open content.js and change the "media" line to match.

Tip: keep each video under ~10 MB so the site stays fast.
(See README.md -> "Make your videos small" for a free way to shrink them.)

Until a file is here, the website shows a stylish placeholder instead.
You can delete this text file once your ads are in.
